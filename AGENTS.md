# Project instructions

Read `WORKFLOW.md` for roles, permissions, commands, verification, Git, and shared-context setup before acting. Read `SPEC.md` for technical requirements and source data, and `PLAN.md` for the assigned task.

## Sources of truth
- `PRODUCT.md`: purpose, audience, and product principles.
- `DESIGN.md`: visual system, component presentation, and motion.
- `SPEC.md`: technical behavior, data/scoring contracts, and unresolved technical choices.
- `WORKFLOW.md`: execution procedures and agent/owner responsibilities.
- `PLAN.md`: task ownership, order, dependencies, and status.
- `MEMORY.md`: observed state and handoff evidence, not requirements or a second task board.
- `DEBT.md`: owner-maintained comprehension notes.

Current owner instructions take precedence over these files. Each topic belongs only in its designated source; link to it elsewhere instead of repeating its rules. External references do not override local decisions. Report contradictions immediately and obtain an owner resolution before dependent work; independent assigned work may continue.

Verify relevant technical guidance online before implementing, recommending, or teaching an approach. Keep personal profiles, contact details, account handles, and machine-specific paths out of project documentation; retain required third-party attribution.
